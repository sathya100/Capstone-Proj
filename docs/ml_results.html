How it was tested

  • Data. DDInter, all 8 downloads (130,422 unique pairs, 2,315 drugs). For this run: the 300 most-connected drugs plus the project's seed drugs, of which 261 have a small-molecule structure in PubChem, giving 16,073 pairs (5,053 Major, 10,528 Moderate, 492 Minor).
  • Features. 1024-bit Morgan fingerprint (radius 2) per drug from its PubChem SMILES; a pair is the sum of its two fingerprints, so drug order never matters.
  • Split by drug. 52 drugs (20%) held out for testing, 26 for validation. Training pairs never contain a test or validation drug: 8,286 train, 2,263 validation, 5,524 test pairs.
  • Safety guard. Tuned on validation: if the model leans Moderate or Minor but gives Major at least 30% probability, the pair is shown as Unknown. Below 60% confidence it is also Unknown, never Low.

Limits of this result

  • Macro F1 is 0.685, short of the 0.70 target, and 86.9% of Majors are shown safely against a 90% target. Training on all 2,315 drugs instead of 261 is the next step and is likely to help.
  • DDInter lists only pairs that interact, so the model has never seen a "no interaction" example and will always predict some level. Its job is to grade severity for an unlisted pair, not to say whether two drugs interact.
  • Biologics such as insulins and antibodies have no fingerprint the model can use well; pairs involving them stay Unknown in the app.
